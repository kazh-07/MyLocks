## Priority Levels

Sample data uses the priority system:
- **Priority 1** (High): Must-visit destinations
- **Priority 2** (Medium): Would like to visit
- **Priority 3** (Low): Someday/maybe

## Notes

All sample data:
- Uses realistic dates (relative to current date)
- Includes descriptive notes for context
- Uses varied companions (Sarah, local friend, business colleagues, solo, partner)
- Shows different rating levels (4-5 stars for restaurants)
- Demonstrates the relationship between visits and wishlists
- Leverages actual restaurants from the MichelinChina2026.csv file

This creates a realistic demo experience for new users!
